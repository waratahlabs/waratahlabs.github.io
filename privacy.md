# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 May 2026**

This privacy policy applies to **Quantum Edge** and other Waratah Labs apps, and to the waratahlabs.com website.

## Apps — No Data Collected

Waratah Labs apps do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Quantum Edge operates entirely on-device. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no network requests to Waratah Labs servers.

All computation — Metal GPU statevector simulation, QAOA circuits, and Apple Intelligence prompt optimisation — runs locally on your device. Nothing leaves your device.

Quantum Edge uses **Apple Intelligence** (FoundationModels framework) on supported devices. Apple's own privacy policy governs how Apple Intelligence processes data. Waratah Labs receives no data from this.

## Website Analytics

The waratahlabs.com website uses [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-respecting analytics platform. Umami collects no personal data and uses no cookies. Aggregate page view data only.
